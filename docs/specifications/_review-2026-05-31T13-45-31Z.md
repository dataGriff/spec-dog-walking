## Important findings

### FIND-001: Missing acceptance scenario for cross-owner walk cancellation

**File + location:** `docs/specifications/acceptance-scenarios.md` (US-009 section)
**Problem:** US-009 (Cancel a walk) has two scenarios: owner cancels own walk (A) and cancel on `completed` walk rejected (B). The auth-matrix specifies cancel allows `walker` (🔒 assigned) and `owner` (🔒 own), meaning an owner cannot cancel another owner's dog's walk — but no scenario tests this cross-owner FORBIDDEN boundary.
**Recommended fix:** Add US-009-C scenario: owner cannot cancel another owner's dog's walk, expecting 403 `FORBIDDEN`.
